# Original imagegen prompts

Built-in image_gen with `transparent_background: true` was called once per actor. The source PNG files are the untouched outputs. The common art brief is an original hand-painted 2D action sprite, front three-quarter view from slightly above, lighting upper-left, red clay, chipped white porcelain, black quarry rock and muted utilitarian bronze, alpha background, exactly four columns by three rows, aligned feet and consistent character. No text, terrain, panel borders or broad ground shadows.

## quarry_cleaver

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game sprite atlas for isometric/top-down angled action game, 4 columns x 3 rows, exactly 12 separate full-body poses of ONE identical character. Subject: quarry_cleaver "Skolorub", stout ceramic clay quarry worker creature, chipped white porcelain head and red-clay torso, dull bronze repair seams, black stone deposits; its singular enormous broad bronze-and-black-stone cleaver clearly held in both hands. Composition: clean strict regular 4x3 grid, same camera, character scale, anatomy and costume in every cell, feet on same baseline in each cell, ample margins and no overlap. Rows: top 4 idle breathing poses; middle 4 walk stride poses; bottom 4 attack poses (raised windup, descending strike, low impact, recovery). Front three-quarter camera from slightly above; default facing right. Weapon remains fully within each cell. Distinct leg and arm silhouettes, no cloned poses. Style: polished hand-painted 2D game sprites with bold readable silhouette and restrained internal texture. Light from upper left. Palette: red clay, white porcelain, black rock, utilitarian muted bronze. Constraints: genuinely transparent background and transparent gutters, no labels, lettering, panel outlines, grid lines, floor, cast shadow, glow, scenery or extra characters.
